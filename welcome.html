Narrative Constructs

Narrative Constructs is a reflexive law engineering lab.

It designs, names, and formalizes the analytical mechanisms and conceptual frameworks used across our legal practice — independently of any single case.

Its output is not evidence. It is method: reusable structures that later get applied, tested, and refined against real situations elsewhere.

Where our clinics turn facts into legible cases, Narrative Constructs builds the tools that make that translation possible in the first place.
